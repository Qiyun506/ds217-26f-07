Line chart of the percentage of patients meeting the exercise goal (%) at follow-up visits 1 to 4, with one line for the home-based program and one for the center-based program. Both rise across the visits: home-based goes from 58% to 70%, and center-based from 57% to 79%, so the two start almost level and center-based finishes 9 points higher at visit 4 (79% against 70%). These are descriptive program summaries; patients chose their program, so the gap does not show that one program causes more exercise. Each percentage also covers only the patients seen at that visit, about 45 per program, so a 9-point gap could also come from chance or from which patients returned.
